How to Apply for a Scholarship

A simple step-by-step guide to help you apply successfully.

📌 Before You Begin

Applying for a scholarship can feel overwhelming, but if you follow the right steps, it becomes easy. Most scholarships in India are applied through government portals like NSP or PFMS, or directly on private organisation websites.

Always read the eligibility criteria carefully before applying. Make sure you qualify before spending time on the application.

1

Search Your Scholarship

Use Scholly India's search and filter feature to find scholarships matching your category, course, income, and academics.

2

Check Eligibility

Read details carefully — eligible categories, minimum academic %, max family income, and eligible courses.

3

Gather Documents

Collect all required documents in advance — income certificate, caste certificate, marksheets, Aadhaar, bank details etc.

4

Register on Portal

Visit the official scholarship portal (NSP, PFMS, or organisation website). Create account using Aadhaar or mobile number.

5

Fill Application Form

Fill all details carefully — personal info, academic records, bank account details. Double-check before submitting.

6

Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of all required documents. Files must be clear and within the allowed size (usually under 200KB).

7

Submit & Take Printout

Submit the form and download the acknowledgement slip. Keep your application number safe for tracking.

8

Track Application

Log in periodically to check your status. Respond promptly to any requests for additional documents.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Apply as early as possible — don't wait for the last day.
  • Keep soft copies of all documents ready on your phone/email.
  • Never pay any fee to apply for a government scholarship — it is always FREE.
  • Make sure your bank account is linked with Aadhaar for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
  • If applying on NSP, get your application verified by your institute's nodal officer.

Documents Required

Keep these documents ready before applying for any scholarship in India.

📋 Why Documents Matter

Scholarship applications require proof of identity, academic performance, income, and category. Preparing documents in advance will save time and prevent rejection. Keep both originals and scanned copies.

If applying under PwD category (issued by CMO)

⚙️ Document Scan Tips

  • Scan all documents at 200 DPI — good clarity, small file size.
  • Most portals accept PDF or JPEG format only.
  • Maximum file size is usually 200KB per document.
  • Ensure text is fully visible — no shadows or cut-off corners.
  • Keep a Google Drive folder with all documents for easy access.

National Scholarship Portal (NSP)

India's largest scholarship platform — managed by the Government of India for students from pre-matric to PhD level.

🏛️

National Scholarship Portal (NSP)

NSP is the One-Stop solution for end-to-end scholarship process for students from pre-matric to PhD level. It covers scholarships from Central Ministries like Minority Affairs, Social Justice, Tribal Affairs, Labour, and more.

NSP is the official Government of India portal under MeitY. It ensures timely disbursement directly to student bank accounts via DBT.

Visit NSP Portal

📋 Scholarships Available on NSP

  • Pre-Matric Scholarship — For SC/ST/OBC/Minority students in Class 9-10
  • Post-Matric Scholarship — For SC/ST/OBC/Minority students in Class 11 and above
  • Merit-cum-Means Scholarship — For Minority students in professional & technical courses
  • Top Class Education Scholarship — For SC students in top-rated institutions
  • National Fellowship — For SC/ST PhD scholars
  • Pragati & Saksham — AICTE scholarships for girl students in technical education

✅ How to Register on NSP

  • Visit scholarships.gov.in and click "New Registration"
  • Select your state, scholarship type, and provide Aadhaar details
  • Verify mobile OTP and create your login credentials
  • Fill in the application form with your academic and personal details
  • Upload required documents and submit
  • Get the application verified by your institute's nodal officer

PFMS – Public Financial Management System

The Government of India's unified payment platform for direct benefit transfers to students.

💳

PFMS Portal

PFMS (Public Financial Management System) is operated by the Controller General of Accounts, Ministry of Finance. It is used for payment of scholarships directly to students' bank accounts through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

If your scholarship amount is being credited via PFMS, you can track your payment status using your bank account number or NSP application number.

Visit PFMS Portal

🔍 How to Track Payment on PFMS

  • Visit pfms.nic.in
  • Click on "Know Your Payment"
  • Select your bank and enter your bank account number
  • Enter the captcha and click "Search"
  • You will see all DBT payments received in your account

⚠️ Important Notes

  • Your bank account must be linked with Aadhaar for DBT to work.
  • Only one bank account can be registered per Aadhaar — make sure it is active.
  • If payment is pending, contact your district nodal officer or NIC helpdesk.
  • PFMS helpline: 1800-111-555 (Toll Free)
  • Contact: Contact form
